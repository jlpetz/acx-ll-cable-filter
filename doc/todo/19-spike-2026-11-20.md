**The loopback's render lead (Astra's review of a66d8ab, 2026-11-23).** The finding: `LoopbackCapturePass` re-anchored at
`anchor - lag - RenderLeadFrames` whenever the render lead changed, so a capture stream moved back and replayed render audio
when it rose, and skipped when it fell. The moves are real; the replay isn't. The lead changes only as a render stream
starts copying (a packet, then 0 once its client releases packets early; `m_LbReleased` is cleared only by ReleaseHardware,
which gives the ring up) and when another render stream claims the ring, and every claim starts a new run (`ValidFrom` =
the claimant's packet). So a rise moves the read back onto silence or onto the new owner's audio, which the stream hasn't
had: time again, never a frame again, and a place that never moves back (a66d8ab's remedy) would have turned the new
owner's first frames into silence. Not done. The fall does skip, the start of the owner's audio (the difference in leads):
a capture stream now keeps the longer lead while the shorter one would skip audio the ring holds, until that audio ends or
